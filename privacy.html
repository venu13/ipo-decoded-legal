This policy explains what information the App handles, why, for how long, and what rights you have. It is written to meet the notice requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules, and Google Play's requirement that apps publish a privacy policy.

1. The short version

  • The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal details.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software, and does not share information with advertisers.
  • Like any app that gets data from a server, each request the App makes to our server is automatically recorded with your IP address and basic technical details (below). We keep those records for 30 days.
  • The summaries in the App are produced from public documents. None of your information is sent to any artificial-intelligence service.

2. What information is handled

Information Where it comes from Why How long
IP address of your device or network Automatically, when the App requests data from our server To deliver the data you asked for; to protect the service from abuse; to diagnose faults 30 days in server logs
The address requested (for example, the IPO list, or one IPO's analysis) and the time of the request Automatically Same as above 30 days in server logs
A label the networking software adds to each request (its name and version, e.g. "okhttp/4.12") Automatically Same as above 30 days in server logs
A copy of recent public IPO subscription figures Downloaded from our server So the App can show data quickly and when the connection is poor On your device only; replaced when refreshed; removed when you uninstall the App (Android's automatic backup of App data is switched off, so it is not copied to a cloud backup)

We do not use this information to identify you, build profiles, or contact you, and we have no way to link a log entry to a named person. The IPO information the App shows is public information about companies and offers, not information about you.

3. What we do not collect

Name, email, phone number, postal address, date of birth, government or financial identifiers, bank or payment details, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising identifiers, or information about other apps on your device. The App requests one Android permission only: access to the internet.

We handle the information in section 2 only to provide the App's features and to keep the service secure and working. Because the App asks you for no personal information and has no optional features that need it, there is no consent screen: using the App means the requests in section 2 are made. 6. Retention

Server logs are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. We keep no other information about you.

Under the DPDP Act you may ask for a summary of any personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, and nominate another person to exercise these rights for you. Because we cannot link log entries to a named person, we may ask for the IP address and time period concerned so we can find any records that relate to you.

8. Children

The App is intended for adults (18 and over). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete any related log entries we can identify.

9. Security

Requests to our server are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to our cloud project is limited to [the operator]. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected people and the authorities of a personal data breach as the law requires.
