This policy explains what information the App handles, why, for how long, and what rights you have. It is written to meet the notice requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules, and Google Play's requirement that apps publish a privacy policy.

1. The short version

  • The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal details.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software, and does not share information with advertisers.
  • Notifications are optional and off until you turn them on. No notification identifier is created until you opt in. If you do, Google's notification service creates a random identifier on your device so it can deliver them (section 2). We never see it.
  • Like any app that gets data from a server, each request the App makes to our server is automatically recorded with your IP address and basic technical details (below). We keep those records for 30 days.
  • The summaries in the App are produced from public documents. None of your information is sent to any artificial-intelligence service.

3. What we do not collect

Name, email, phone number, postal address, date of birth, government or financial identifiers, bank or payment details, precise or approximate location (your IP address is recorded in server logs as described in section 2, but we do not use it to work out where you are), contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, advertising identifiers, or information about other apps on your device.

The app requests only the standard Android permissions listed below. Libraries it uses may also add a small internal permission that protects the app's own components; it gives no access to your data or device.

  • INTERNET: to get IPO information from our server.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (only if you opt in): to show notifications. Android 13 and later asks you for this separately, and the App asks only if you choose to turn on notifications. You can refuse or withdraw it in your phone's settings at any time; if you do, the App turns its notification settings off.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: lets the App check whether the phone is online. Added by Google's notification software.
  • WAKE_LOCK: lets the phone stay awake briefly while a notification arrives. Added by Google's notification software.
  • Google messaging receive permission (com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE): lets the App receive messages sent through Google's notification service. Added by Google's notification software.

The last three are used only so that notifications can arrive, and they do nothing unless you turn notifications on. The App does not use the advertising identifier, and the notification identifier is not one.

4. Who else handles information

  • Google (cloud hosting). Our server runs on Google Cloud (Firebase / Cloud Run / Firestore), which processes the requests described above on our behalf under Google's terms.
  • Google (AI processing of public documents). To write the summaries, our server sends the text of public offer documents to Google's Gemini service. No information about you or your device is included in those requests, and they are not made when you use the App.
  • Google (notifications). If you turn on notifications, Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging service delivers them. Google creates the notification identifier on your device and acts as our processor for delivery: we send each notification to a topic, and Google sends it on to the devices subscribed to that topic. Nothing about you is sent to us. Google's handling of the identifier is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Play. If you install the App from Google Play, Google separately collects information under its own privacy policy. We do not control this and do not receive it in identifiable form.
  • We do not sell or rent information, and we do not disclose it to anyone else, except where the law requires us to.

5. Purpose and legal basis

We handle the information in section 2 only to provide the App's features and to keep the service secure and working. The App asks you for no personal information. For the requests in section 2 other than notifications there is no consent screen: using the App means those requests are made. Notifications are different: they are optional and run only if you turn them on in the App (which also triggers Android's own permission prompt). Your consent is that choice, and you can withdraw it by turning the setting off in About or by refusing or withdrawing the permission in your phone's settings.

6. Retention

Server logs are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. When you turn all alerts off, the app deletes its registration token. Google may keep the installation record until the app is uninstalled or inactive for a long time. Turning an alert off also ends its topic subscription. We keep no other information about you.

7. Deleting your data

IPO Decoded, operated by Venugopal B V, keeps very little about you. You can delete what exists in any of these ways:

  • Turn off all alerts in the app's About screen. This deletes the app's notification registration token.
  • Uninstall the app. This removes all data stored on your device.
  • Email ipodecoded.support@gmail.com to ask about records relating to you.

Deleted: the notification registration token when alerts are turned off, and the data on your device when the app is uninstalled.

Kept: server logs (IP address, requested address, time and request label) for 30 days, then deleted automatically. Google may keep the installation record until the app is uninstalled or inactive for a long time.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask for a summary of any personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, and nominate another person to exercise these rights for you. Because we cannot link log entries to a named person, we may ask for the IP address and time period concerned so we can find any records that relate to you.

To exercise a right or make a complaint, write to ipodecoded.support@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

The App is intended for adults (18 and over). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete any related log entries we can identify.

10. Security

Requests to our server are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to our cloud project is limited to Venugopal B V. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected people and the authorities of a personal data breach as the law requires.

11. Changes

If we change what the App handles (for example, adding accounts or analytics) we will update this policy before the change takes effect and show the new date above.
