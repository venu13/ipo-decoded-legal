1. What the App does

The App sets out, in plainer words, what public IPO documents say. It draws on:

  • the Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) and related offer documents that companies file publicly with SEBI and the stock exchanges, and
  • public subscription data published by the National Stock Exchange of India.

For each IPO it shows the filing's business description, reported financial figures, stated use of funds, promoter holding and related-party transactions, and risk factors the filing lists, together with short summaries. The summaries and explanations are written with the help of automated tools, including artificial intelligence, working only from those documents. Glossary entries explain general terms and are not about any particular company.

The App is informational. It is not a course, a research service, or an advisory service.

3. Accuracy of information

  • The App reports what the source documents say. Those documents are the authoritative source; the App's version is a convenience.
  • Automated summarisation can make mistakes, leave things out, or misread a table. We apply automated checks (for example, that figures shown appear in the source text), but these checks do not guarantee accuracy or completeness.
  • Filings can be amended, and figures can be restated after we have processed a document. Subscription figures are delayed and may differ from the exchange's live data.
  • Where a figure or fact matters to you, check it against the offer document.
